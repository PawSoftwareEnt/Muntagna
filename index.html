The Story

At her foot nobody calls her Etna: she is a Muntagna, the Mountain. Agata promised her grandfather Turi she would take him up there one last time, to the crater. So she sets out from Nicolosi, the town at its foot, with his urn in her backpack.

She talks to him as she climbs. On the way are the people who knew him, the places he used to bring her, and a mountain that is very much alive.

“You made me promise I'd take you all the way up there. To the crater.” Agata

The Game

A precision platformer in pixel art, one screen at a time, from the roofs of the town up to the crater.

01

Jump, climb, dash

Pixel-exact controls: a jump you can shape, walls to climb while your grip lasts, and a dash in eight directions.

02

One room, one idea

Every screen is made by hand around one thing: quarry slabs that bolt when you step on them, embers that give your dash back in mid-air, fumaroles that throw you upward.

03

Fall, and go again

It is hard, and it does not waste your time: you start again at once, in the same room.

04

A real mountain

Etna's skyline, as you see it from the south, over every open-air room, and the churches of Nicolosi drawn from photographs.

05

Worth the detour

Prickly pears hidden in rooms of their own, Grandpa's postcards, pistachios for Aunt Pina's cake, and one secret in every chapter.

06

Your way

In Italian and English, with keyboard or controller, controls you can remap and assists such as infinite lives.
