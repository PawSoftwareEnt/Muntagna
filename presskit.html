About the game

Muntagna is a precision platformer about a climb up Mount Etna, made of cut paper.

Agata promised her grandfather Turi she would take him all the way up to the crater. Now she carries his ashes in her backpack and his red scarf round her neck, and she sets off from Nicolosi, the town at the foot of the Mountain where the climb begins.

The free demo is the whole first chapter: the town, from its square to its rooftops.

What makes it its own

  • A wingbeat, not a dash: a blue rock thrush rides on her backpack and gives her one push in the air.
  • Walls you slide down, not climb: kick off them to go up.
  • The red scarf swings her from the iron rings of the old mule tracks.
  • Grandpa's walking stick bounces her off prickly pear pads.
  • Sicily, not a fantasy land: a real town under a real volcano, a dog on its chain, wheels of fire.
  • One room, one idea: when you fall, you are back at once.
  • Off the way: prickly pears, postcards, pistachios and a secret.
